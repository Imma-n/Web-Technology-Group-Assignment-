About Us

Built by technicians, run for clients.

Our History

BrightTech Solutions was founded in 2014 by a small team of hardware technicians who were tired of watching local businesses get stuck with equipment they didn't need and support they couldn't reach. What started as a two-person repair shop has grown into a full-service technology partner, offering hardware supply, custom software, networking, and IT support to businesses across the region.

Over the past decade, we've expanded from fixing individual machines to designing entire office networks and building software that runs behind the scenes of our clients' daily operations. Every service we've added exists because a client asked for it.

Our Mission

To give businesses of every size access to reliable, well-supported technology — without the jargon, the upsells, or the wait.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted IT partner in the region, known as much for how we treat clients as for the systems we build.

How We Work

Every engagement starts with listening. We assess what a client already has, what's actually causing them pain, and what a realistic budget looks like — then we recommend hardware, software, or networking changes that solve the real problem, not just the symptom.
